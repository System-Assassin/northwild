# Northwild — Survival Design Brief

## Identity

Northwild is a hardcore, open-ended, single-player bushcraft survival game set around Femundsmarka, Norway. Nature is the antagonist. There are no zombies, supernatural enemies or arbitrary levelling bonuses. Knowledge, preparation and sound decisions keep the player alive.

## Design pillars

1. **Bushcraft is a process.** Fire, shelter and water preparation require the correct materials and sequence.
2. **Weather changes the plan.** Wind, rain, snow, daylight and wet equipment matter more than an oversized health bar.
3. **Readable realism.** The simulation should be deep, but the player must be able to understand why their condition is changing.
4. **No chosen-one story.** The world continues indefinitely and the player creates their own goals.
5. **Knowledge replaces grinding.** Better technique unlocks efficiency; repetition does not magically increase axe damage.

## Target survival model

- Core and skin temperature, wind chill, clothing insulation and wetness
- Calories by macronutrient, hydration and water contamination
- Fatigue, sleep quality, illness, wounds, infection and pain
- Fire triangle: heat, oxygen and fuel; material surface area and moisture
- Shelter insulation, ground conduction, wind direction and precipitation cover
- Tool edge condition, handles, sharpening, repair and safe use
- Renewable and finite resources with seasonal availability
- Wildlife behaviour driven by territory, hunger, fear, scent and time of day
- Navigation through terrain, compass, sun, stars and a physical map

## Vertical slice

The first playable area is a measured 1 km² crop of the western shore and islands of Nedre Roasten, with conifer/birch woodland around the cold lake. It proves:

- first/third-person movement;
- gathering and weighted inventory;
- raw and safe water;
- staged fire construction and ignition;
- fuel burn, rain exposure and heat radius;
- lean-to shelter protection;
- accelerated day/night and dynamic weather;
- survival vitals, death, saving and loading.

## Next milestones

### Milestone 2 — Hands and tools

Add physical item models, pickup/placement, axe and knife use, chopping blocks, carving, splitting and sharpening. Replace key-driven fire preparation with direct interaction.

### Milestone 3 — Terrain and ecology

Build a streamed Scandinavian biome with lakes, bogs, exposed high ground and dense forest. Add flora identification, foraging, fishing and small-game trapping.

### Milestone 4 — Exposure and medicine

Add layered clothing, localised wetness, sleep, wounds, fractures, burns, illness and a grounded treatment system.

### Milestone 5 — Wildlife and long-term survival

Add prey, predators, food preservation, larger shelters, seasonal change, resource regeneration and a long-term sandbox balance pass.
